# Limitations

This is an interview case study, not an Elexon monitoring product.

1. The data is generated with a fixed seed. It is not Elexon telemetry.
2. The Circular does not disclose private arrival rates, queue data, capacity headroom or lower level technical cause.
3. Alert thresholds and capacity are demonstration settings. They are not BSC limits, Elexon service levels or DCP limits.
4. The queue model assumes steady rates and does not model internal dependencies, recovery behaviour or architecture.
5. Participant and workload patterns demonstrate investigation method. They do not assign responsibility.
6. A forecast difference does not prove a poor forecasting process.
7. The dashboard does not authorise a pause, a payment delay or a customer instruction.
8. FMAR is consultation material and draft API context. It is not evidence that FMAR is the affected DCP service.
9. Power BI is not run or tested in this repository. CSV exports and model guidance are provided for a later Power BI build.
10. Real production use would require agreed metric definitions, access controls, data lineage, operational ownership, service objectives and customer impact review.
