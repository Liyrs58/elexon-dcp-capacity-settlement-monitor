# Elexon values in the design

## We are one team

The impact page identifies the service owner, Settlement operations, the relevant provider and customers. The analyst keeps the issue moving across those groups.

## We think customer first

The project measures possible Settlement and payment effects and asks what a customer should do. It does not treat a technical chart as the outcome.

## We work at pace

The validation controls and early warning state help the analyst reach a safe first view quickly. Deeper investigation can continue without delaying a necessary update.

## We think beyond

The hypotheses, scenarios and improvement register ask what may prevent recurrence. FMAR is mentioned as future context, not as a claimed fix.

## We focus on what matters

The executive view leads with queue, throughput, forecast variance, latency and critical run status. Detail is available only when it helps an investigation or decision.
